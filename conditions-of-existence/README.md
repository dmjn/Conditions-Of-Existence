# Conditions of Existence

An interactive atlas of artificial life as worldmaking, part of the Worldmaking Project by Damjan Jovanovic.

Artificial life changed worldmaking from the composition of a whole into the establishment of conditions through which a whole can develop and come to exceed its maker's anticipation. The atlas runs five of the field's worlds live in the page, each with its conditions exposed as controls:

- **The Game of Life** (Conway, 1970): presets for the Gosper glider gun, gliders, the R-pentomino and random soup, with a brush for stamping gliders, R-pentominoes or single cells
- **Boids** (Reynolds, 1987): weights for collision avoidance, velocity matching and flock centering, a perception radius, and one boid's field of perception drawn on the flock
- **Evolved creatures** (after Sims, 1994): jointed bodies evolved against a fitness function chosen by the reader, including the peak-height loophole recorded by Lehman and colleagues
- **Tierra** (after Ray, 1990): a schematic soup of hosts, parasites and immune hosts, drawn either as a memory map or as bodies
- **Daisyworld** (Watson and Lovelock, 1983): the original equations, with a luminosity slider, a sweep of the sun and switches to remove either species

The opening dish runs Particle Life after Jeffrey Ventrella's Clusters, and a section on the contemporary field links to ALIEN, Computational Life, Growing Neural Cellular Automata, Lenia and Flow-Lenia, Clusters and Particle Life, The Bibites, the xenobots and ASAL.

The creature physics and the Tierra soup are models written for this atlas and simplify their sources; each lab says how.

## Running

Static HTML and JavaScript with no build step. Open `index.html` through any web server, or publish with GitHub Pages (Settings → Pages → Deploy from a branch → `main`, folder `/`). Fonts load from Google Fonts.
